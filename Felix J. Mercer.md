# Felix James Mercer

**Name:** Felix James Mercer
**Born:** 1963, Bellhaven, Arentian Republic
**Gender:** Male
**Nationality:** Arentian-Cruzian
**Occupation:** Civil Engineer, Entrepreneur; Owner of Newbridge Construction Ltd. and Strata Engineering & Feasibility Ltd.

## Background

Felix James Mercer was born in Bellhaven, Arentian Republic, to an Arentian father and a mother of Rhenic descent, both employed by an Arentian engineering company. Shortly after Cruzia's Liberation in 1979, his family relocated to the country to pursue emerging opportunities in its recovering industrial and infrastructure sectors.

Mercer subsequently completed his education in Cruzia and pursued a career in civil engineering and construction management. In 1990, he founded **[Newbridge Construction Ltd.](https://discord.com/channels/1503937565135208490/1559995757715529789)**, followed by **[Strata Engineering & Feasibility Ltd.](https://discord.com/channels/1503937565135208490/1559996325838192700)** in 1996. Through both companies, he has established himself in Cruzia's construction and engineering consultancy sectors.
