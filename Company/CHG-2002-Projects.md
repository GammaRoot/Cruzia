Chuyoda Hospitality Group (CHG) is planning two inland hospitality developments and would like to involve **Strata in feasibility and engineering assessment, and Newbridge in construction planning and delivery**. Capacities below are proposed starting points, subject to feasibility.

**Kalahooska country resort — feasibility and initial construction targeted for 2002**

- Approximately **64 guest units:** 48 hotel rooms and 16 detached cottages.
- Main hotel with reception, restaurant, bar, lounges and private dining.
- Flexible events room for approximately **100–120 guests**, plus smaller meeting rooms.
- Landscaped grounds, walking routes, a modest pool and treatment facilities.
- Potential collaboration with Kalahooska Fine Tobacco through estate excursions, hosted events and hospitality supply. The hotel would occupy a separately selected site; KFT estate land is not assumed available.
- Strata’s scope would cover site comparison, ground conditions, foundations, drainage, access, utilities and environmental constraints. Newbridge would contribute construction estimates and sequencing.
- Target: feasibility early in 2002, followed by site preparation, drainage, utility works and foundations later that year, subject to land arrangements, approvals, funding and agreement. Main construction would continue into 2003.

**Highland lodge — feasibility in 2002; construction targeted for 2003**

- Approximately **36 guest units:** 24 lodge rooms and 12 detached cabins.
- Main lodge with reception, restaurant, lounge and a retreat/meeting room for approximately **30–40 people**.
- Walking routes, outdoor-equipment storage, drying rooms and modest sauna/treatment facilities.
- Strata’s assessment should particularly examine terrain stability, foundation requirements, road access, drainage, utility provision, weather exposure and seasonal construction constraints.
- Newbridge’s construction planning would follow the site findings, with commencement targeted for 2003.

Both concepts should use compact layouts with provision for later expansion. Neither includes a golf course, large convention centre or major spa complex. Sites, budgets, final designs, contracts and completion dates remain to be agreed.
