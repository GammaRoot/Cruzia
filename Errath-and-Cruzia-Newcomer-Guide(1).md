# Welcome to Errath and Cruzia

*A newcomer’s guide to the public setting of Cruzia RP, set around 2001.*

This guide covers public background, established events and ordinary commercial life. A player section explains participation outside the fiction. Chuyoda internal records, confidential character information and GM-only lore are excluded. Historical figures keep their original dates; plans and announcements are not treated as completed results.

Start with the overview, regions and current issues, then use the detailed sections as a reference. You do not need to memorize every name before joining.

## The setting at a glance

**Errath** is the world. **Cruzia** is the island country at the centre of this RP: a diverse constitutional monarchy off the southwestern coast of Valdoria, positioned beside important international shipping routes.

Cruzia overthrew a communist dictatorship in 1979 and has spent the following two decades building its democracy. By 2001, its main concerns include a severe economic depression, the future of domestic industry, strained foreign relations, an overseas military campaign, and growing crime. Regional identity, religion, indigenous rights, and memories of dictatorship all shape political life.

The setting has its own countries, cultures, and history. Familiar political ideas and institutions exist, but real-world events do not automatically happen here.

## Errath: the wider world

### Two moons and five continents

Errath has two moons: **Lyra**, the larger and slower moon, and **Seren**, the smaller and faster one. Their combined influence creates complex tides. The **Confluence** is a recurring alignment associated with unusually high tides and cultural or religious observances. It matters particularly to coastal communities and seafarers.

### The history behind international politics

Valdorian empires once controlled territories across Errath. The **Solentine Empire** was especially influential in Cruzia's settlement, institutions, and maritime connections.

The **Great War, 1914–1945**, was one prolonged catastrophe with interruptions, rather than two separate world wars. It included the Kovari Revolution, the rise of the fascist Nationalist Compact, mass persecution and murder of the Ehari people, and an atomic ending.

After 1945, the **Cold Division** set the Arentian Republic and Kovari Union at the head of rival blocs. Nuclear deterrence, proxy conflicts, aid, trade, and struggles over political alignment shaped smaller countries' choices. This history explains much of Cruzia's foreign-policy debate. By the early 2000s, the Kovari Union is reformist; descriptions of its 1980 invasion of Durghan concern an earlier, now-ended war.

### How government works

The monarchy is ceremonial. Empress Autumn is a national figure, while elected institutions exercise political authority. Parliament makes laws; the President, Prime Minister, and ministers occupy the principal executive offices. Regional governors are also important political actors.

Constitutional rights, parliamentary votes, elections, court decisions, coalition negotiations, and confidence votes all matter. Political officeholders can disagree sharply even while serving within the same government.

Kosta Piastri is also known as a four-time Formula One champion. His **One Nation** party emerged in the late 1990s with a nationalist, pro-tariff, anti-League platform. Political arguments about industry, sovereignty, employment, and foreign influence are central to the current period.

## People, faith, and identity

Cruzia's population reflects indigenous settlement, colonialism, and migration. Common community names include:

- **Heritage Cruzians:** people with longstanding Kraivic roots, forming the largest ethnic community.
- **Aravani:** the indigenous people of Cruzia, with their own language, spiritual traditions, and continuing land and cultural concerns.
- **Valdric-Cruzians:** a community particularly associated with Piranburgh's industrial and commercial history.
- **Solari-Cruzians:** descendants of settlers from Solentine and related western Valdorian backgrounds, especially visible along the southern coast.
- **Volgric-Cruzians:** a community especially associated with the Volga Corridor and eastern Valdorian heritage.
- **Halvari:** people of mixed Kraivic, Solari, and Aravani ancestry.
- **Ehari-Cruzians:** part of Errath's wider Ehari diaspora, with longstanding communities in Cruzian urban life.

Incheon and Myrati refugees also form part of the country's more recent migration history. Community background does not determine a person's loyalties, religion, occupation, or politics.

The major religious traditions are:

- **The Concordat:** a monotheistic faith associated with the Prophet Arvan. Its Roman, Eastern, and Reformed branches all have a substantial Cruzian presence.
- **Aravani spiritual traditions:** varied practices linking the moons, the ocean, ancestors, and communal memory, transmitted through oral tradition and ritual.
- **The Ehari faith:** an old covenantal monotheistic tradition closely connected with Ehari peoplehood and diaspora identity.
- **The Veil:** a faith widespread in Surath and coastal Orethia, also practised by smaller communities in Cruzia.

Secular and atheist Cruzians are also part of national life. Religious institutions played important roles in resistance to the dictatorship, giving them an enduring public presence. Their influence and political positions vary.

## What is happening around 2001?

**Economic hardship is a national concern.** The Depression has damaged industry and employment. Foreign ownership, trade protections, welfare, banking, and rebuilding productive capacity are major political questions. Retirement age has risen to seventy, with existing pensioners protected, and unemployment benefits have a six-month limit alongside state job programmes.

**Foreign alignment remains contentious.** Arentian military access continues. Relations with the Valdorian League are severely strained. Arguments over national independence and outside influence carry the weight of both colonial and dictatorship-era experience.

**The Myrati campaign is ongoing.** Cruzia has intervened following Black Tide's invasion. Liberation has progressed, but the campaign has not ended. Refugees and the demands of military involvement affect life at home.

**Crime is a growing public concern.** Communities, businesses, and authorities face increasing public-security pressures amid the Depression. Conditions differ between regions.

**Business is part of public life.** Names you may encounter include Chuyoda and its vehicle manufacturer CMC; the Boreal group, with automotive, telecommunications, banking, shipbuilding, and other businesses; and Femboy Defense Systems, or FDS, a defence manufacturer. Domestic production, jobs, infrastructure, and government purchasing connect business activity with politics.

## Politics and government in more detail

### The Crown, government and Parliament

The House of Laurier represents the constitutional monarchy established after Liberation. Empress Autumn’s speeches can carry national significance, but elected institutions authorize government action. During the Myrati crisis, her total-war proclamation was symbolic; Parliament voted to protect Myrati.

The President and Prime Minister are separate offices. The President does not automatically command agreement from Parliament, ministers or governors. Confidence votes and coalition negotiations can change the government between elections. The Constitutional Court has intervened in contested electoral results.

Parliament is the lawmaking institution. For players, this means the active **Player Parliament**. A historical NPC National Assembly is not a second operative legislative chamber. Parliamentary size varies with elections and participation: an old reference to eleven seats does not describe a permanent chamber size. Regional constituencies and party-list representation both matter.

Some people hold several offices. Poly is Prime Minister, governor of Piranburgh and its constituency MP; Stressedcarrot is Frontier governor and constituency MP. The President and Vice President cannot also hold MP seats. A seat with nobody elected does not acquire an invented officeholder.

### The 2000 election and government formation

Kosta Piastri defeated Poly in the presidential election. Reported turnout was **66.7%**, with **22.81 million** voters from **34.2 million** eligible. Kosta received **60.6% of continuing presidential ballots**: approximately 11.68 million votes against Poly’s 7.60 million. Approximately 3.54 million Protest votes exhausted rather than transferring to a candidate.

A no-confidence vote removed Stressedcarrot as Prime Minister. Kosta’s proposed appointment of Adriaana Piastri was rejected, and Poly subsequently became PM. Adeline Piastri is the recorded Vice President. The executive is therefore not simply a list of people belonging to the President’s party.

### Rights, violence and constitutional change

The first free election returned Piran as President and Shape Talarico as Prime Minister. The 1981 Constitution established the ceremonial monarchy, rights and anti-coup provisions. Aravani suffrage came in 1983, followed by full electoral participation in 1984: Liberation did not immediately provide equal political participation for every community.

An amendment in 1984 changed annual terms to biennial terms from 1986. The Veshkara Charter was ratified in 1985. In 1986, Article 7.4’s Aravani land provisions were revived; the Land Justice Commission completed their execution in 1987. Indigenous rights are constitutional and historical questions as well as current political concerns.

The period also saw serious violence and minority persecution. **Black July** killed 47 people in 1986. Keharat’s **Reckoning** killed 40 NPC legislators, and martial law followed in Piranburgh. Article 22.5’s Ehari-Cruzian protection was repealed by referendum, followed by approximately 10,600 Ehari emigrants. This history informs contemporary arguments about security, religion and civil rights.

**Keharat** is a movement originating around the 1890s, with Establishment/Diaspora divisions. It is distinct from the state of Kedar. Neither label makes all Ehari people responsible for its actions.

The 1986 settlement included the Military Code of Justice, War Powers Act, Civil Code and Criminal Code. Later legislation addressed collective bargaining, dismissal protection, youth courts and legal fees. Politics has developed through courts and lawmaking as well as campaigns and protests.

Cresia won 66.3% in 1986 and 82% in 1988. His assassination on **1 January 1990** was a major national rupture. This guide assigns no perpetrator identity.

The 1996 presidential election was disputed. Mezata’s result was voided over C.Shep voter fraud, a Constitutional Court partial recount followed, and Penguin and Weaoh emerged as the winning presidential ticket. Piran was Prime Minister after that election, rather than President.

Piranburgh martial law ended in 1997. That year also brought the tariff-elimination decision whose aftermath dominates the following period.

## Work, money and the economic crisis

### From growth to depression

Before the crash, public economic records described substantial expansion. Reported GDP was **Ƶ342.5 billion in 1986**, **Ƶ736.5 billion in 1994** and **Ƶ931.71 billion in 1997**. Reported unemployment was **5.6% in 1994** and **4.8% in 1997**. The first surplus was recorded in 1989, and the sixth consecutive surplus in 1997.

These are historical figures, not measures of conditions in 2001. The 49.2-million population figure likewise belongs to 1994. No later census, current GDP, average wage or cost-of-living figure is supplied here.

Cresia’s public economic legacy is associated with protectionism and export-led industrial development. The 1997 **Cruzia–Valdorian League Tariff Elimination Act**, authored by Poly and passed with PM Piran breaking a tie, changed that trading relationship.

The Depression began in 1998. Industrial closures and acquisitions, layoffs, weakened exports, pressure on the Zlat, imported inflation, rising welfare needs and lower tax receipts became central public concerns. Piranburgh’s industrial collapse is especially important. Debate increasingly centres on domestic production, employment and harmful foreign control.

A factory, farm, port or bank can therefore be directly relevant to national politics. A promised expansion or job programme attracts attention, but its announcement does not establish employment or recovery.

### Laws and institutions responding to the crisis

After the AFC victory in 1998, Parliament passed measures addressing foreign acquisitions, trade and finance.

| Measure                            | Practical significance                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **FISA**                           | Requires prior authorization for foreign acquisitions in critical sectors and permits review of coordinated market destruction/acquisition patterns.                                                                                                 |
| **FAT Tax**                        | Qualifying foreign-acquisition transactions face a 10% base, 25% strategic, 35% insolvency/liquidation or 40% opportunistic-distress rate. The highest applicable rate applies; rates do not stack. New capital and small passive stakes are exempt. |
| **Foreign Trade and Payments Act** | Part of the post-crisis trade and financial framework.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Independent Central Bank Act**   | Establishes central-bank independence; Section 9.2 prohibits monetization.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Cabinet Duties Act**             | Part of the institutional response.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Trade Remedies Act**             | Provides a route for trade-remedy investigations. The steel/cement and related investigation has no established final determination here.                                                                                                            |

FISA’s regulated sectors include infrastructure, technology, heavy industry, critical-input supply chains, media and sensitive data. The law does not mean every foreign business is banned or every disputed acquisition has been reversed.

The **Banking Supervision Authority** approves bank control changes. A deposit guarantee exists. The **Y2K remediation gap** remains unresolved; particular bank failures or a completed post-mortem should not be assumed.

The **Cruzian Federal Bank**, **Boreal Bank of Cruzia** and the privately held **Piranburgh Merchant Bank / Bank of Cruzia** are different institutions. Similar names do not make them interchangeable.

### Welfare, employment and infrastructure

Retirement age is **seventy**, with existing pensioners protected. Unemployment benefits are capped at **six months**, alongside state job programmes. These measures were passed and in force in 2000.

The cap makes prolonged unemployment politically urgent. Poly met union representatives with the labour minister and ordered an assessment of workers approaching the deadline, transition proposals and Piranburgh job support. This does not establish that a general benefit extension passed.

Zieliński’s public priorities include rural roads and bridges, water management, irrigation, agricultural storage, machinery access and training. He favours maintenance, credible lifecycle costs and transparent procurement. These are his priorities; individual projects and awards still need established outcomes.

Cruzia has had a nuclear industry since the early 1990s. Earlier reactor plans and priorities do not prove every proposed reactor has opened. Exact contemporary energy shares are not supplied here.

## Regional life and public programmes

The regions offer different starting points for a character. Their historical identities provide context rather than prescribing every resident’s views.

**New Cruzia City** connects national government, finance, media and commerce. Parliamentary politics, reporting, administration and banking repeatedly bring participants to NCC.

**Mezata Plains** makes farm incomes, machinery, transport links and rural representation especially relevant. Its governor is C.Shep and constituency MP Kris Piastri. The Foundry Bridge Programme remains conditional, with funding undecided; it is not a completed recovery project.

**Piranburgh** ties manufacturing and ports to employment, organized labour and trade policy. Poly’s overlapping offices connect local and national debates. His proposed regional constitution was ruled unconstitutional; a narrower charter remained an intention.

**Kalahooska Valley** provides farming, market-town and administrative settings. Paul Piastri is its elected constituency MP, while no governor was elected in 2000. Kalahooska Fine Tobacco is a region-associated commercial name.

**Río Mestizo Coast** connects dock work, fishing, shipping, shipbuilding, tourism and coastal life. Boreal Shipbuilding and Boreal Petrochemical Refineries are headquartered there; Chuyoda Shores Resort and Dale Racetrack are also coastal venues.

**Highland Interior** combines Aravani land and cultural questions with forestry, mining, agriculture and development. Its capital was named **Madinat Sirin-Zilts-Wayau** in 1999. A small Veil community was recorded around three mining clusters by 1997. Refugee arrivals make the Myrati campaign an immediate local concern.

**Frontier Territories** connects outlying communities and small farms with the Boreal businesses based in **Frontier’s End**. Stressedcarrot’s public Job Relief Fund received a reported one-off **Ƶ500 million**: **Ƶ100 million** for farmers and the rest for commercial/industrial relief. Annual-contribution pledges and a 10% unemployment target by 2005 are promises, not achieved results.

Frontier’s Technical Training Programme specifies **Ƶ75 million a year** and a target of **25,000–30,000 trainees**, prioritizing unemployed residents aged 18–30 and including refugees. These are parameters, not achieved throughput. The reopening of the longstanding Frontier Industrial & Mercantile Co. was underway; completion should not be assumed.

**Volga Corridor** provides a river-valley setting with fishing, cooperative agriculture and Volgric-Cruzian and Eastern Concordat traditions. No governor or constituency MP was elected there in the recorded 2000 result.

## Wars, diplomacy and displaced communities

### Myrati

Myrati is an island kingdom independent since **1959**, with its capital at **Petra** and a royal branch of the House of Laurier. Its islands include **Myrat**, **Sael**, **Korin**, the **Tern Islands** and **Vessa Rock**.

Black Tide invaded in 1999. The first recorded refugee wave brought approximately **forty vessels and 6,500 people** to Highland’s coast, handled by Weaoh and Ilena Kastel. Refugees spoke an older Cruzian dialect. Craișe contributed **Ƶ20 million** and established charities.

Parliament voted to defend Myrati. The campaign includes the Sael landing and staged liberation of Myrat. After the recorded Myrat liberation milestone, Pengwing held naval command, Mikhail Karaj army command and Kosta final authority. Adeline’s recorded diplomatic remit was Myrati.

The campaign remains ongoing around 2001. Liberating Myrat does not mean liberating every island or ending the war. Myrati has not become an established annexed part of Cruzia.

### Incheon

Incheon displacement is an earlier, separate story. Black Tide’s invasion there began in the 1970s. Refugees subsequently reached Cruzia, including undocumented boat arrivals; agricultural and industrial work feature in their settlement history.

Cruzia refused an Incheon delegation’s demand to stop accepting refugees in 1990. The 1998 AFC government was receptive on anti-slavery and diaspora grounds. **Drift**, led by **Jaegyeon Na**, is another Incheon-related name a newcomer may encounter.

Black Tide and the broadly recognized **Republic of the Incheon Isles** are distinct powers. Cruzia had no recorded official position on that regime in the supplied 2001 account. Detailed Incheon events are not reconstructed beyond the supported Cruzian context.

Myrati refugees, the previously settled Myrati community and Incheon refugees are different populations. Their estimates should not be combined into a new census total.

### Kedar, Kethari and international institutions

**Kedar** is the Ehari homeland founded in 1948 in western Orethia. The **Kethari** are stateless coastal western Orethians practising the Veil, with contested status in Kedar.

The **Great West Orethian War** began in 1997 following Kedar’s assassination of Kethari leadership and the entry of Veil-faith Orethian states into a multi-front war. It remains active in 2001. Cruzia is not a belligerent, although pro-Kedar lobbies and domestic arguments about the conflict exist. An entire diaspora should not be equated with one government’s policies.

The **International Court of Arbitration (ICA)** is seated in the Sunari Archipelago. **Kelapapura** is the recorded SCC headquarters city; **Aldenmoor** is the Solentine Dominion’s capital.

The Arentian access agreement approved in 1986 remains in place. One Nation’s election does not itself revoke it. Relations with the League remain paused or severed following the crisis; recovery does not automatically imply renewed accession.

## Getting started as a player (OOC)

**Cruzia+ is a Discord political simulation and RP set on Errath. Herta is the GM.** This section explains participation outside the fiction and supplies no hidden setting answers.

### Choose a manageable starting point

Choose a region, background, occupation and immediate concern. You can begin with a local problem without needing a position on every international dispute.

Possible starting points include a Piranburgh worker concerned about employment, a Mezata farmer seeking transport or machinery access, an NCC political organizer, a Río Mestizo shipping employee or a Highland community representative. These are character ideas, not newly established people or events.

Connect a character to an institution within its accepted authority and access. Confirm officeholding, ownership, extraordinary abilities or privileged knowledge through the appropriate RP process.

### Knowledge and actions

Public history, election results, laws, ordinary businesses and publicly introduced events provide common context. Personal experience may give a character more knowledge of a field; reading an OOC document does not grant access to confidential records.

Distinguish reports, beliefs and confirmed facts. Characters can misunderstand events or disagree about policy. Rumours remain rumours unless the story establishes them.

State what your character or organization attempts, why, and what authority or resources it uses. A proposal or offer gives others something to answer; it does not automatically establish agreement.

- A politician can advocate a bill; passage requires the legislative process.
- A company can offer equipment; procurement and acceptance need the appropriate authority.
- A governor can announce a programme; the announcement does not prove payments, construction or jobs.
- A military participant can attempt an operation; consequential outcomes require adjudication.

Leave other player characters’ decisions to their players. Research, implementation and effects are separate steps. A date passing does not itself complete a project or resolve a dispute.

### Elections and events

Follow the current posted ballot and campaign instructions. The RP distinguishes actual player ballots from a separately modeled electorate. The 2000 national and constituency elections used a 50/50 blend; party-list voting used player ballots. Historical rules provide context; check the instructions for the election you enter.

Campaign actions and promises matter. Money supports campaign capacity rather than automatically buying vote share. Missed promises and deadlines can affect credibility.

**Shadows Over Cruzia — Rise of the Underworld** is an active 2001 national event. Its public premise is growing crime amid hardship. Smuggling, theft, black-market trade and extortion existed before the Depression; the crisis aggravated them. This premise supplies no list of specific organizations, culprits or resolved incidents.

For a decision situation, check eligibility, deadline, resources and mutually exclusive choices. Suggested choices usually include a custom-action route. Under this event’s rules, no response defaults to Ignore unless specified otherwise; inaction can have consequences. Cooperation may matter, and attempted actions still require adjudication.

### Common points of confusion

1. **Errath is the world; Cruzia is one country within it.**
2. **The setting is around 2001; real-world calendar changes do not establish fictional events.**
3. **The 1994 population is a dated reference, not a 2001 census.**
4. **Penguin and Pengwing are different people; Chuyoda and Minister Zieliński are different people.**
5. **Myrati’s campaign, the Great West Orethian War and Incheon displacement are separate contexts.**
