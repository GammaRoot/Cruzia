## Description

**Strata Engineering & Feasibility Ltd.** is a privately owned Cruzian engineering consultancy established in 1996 by Felix James Mercer. Based in New Cruzia City, the company specializes in evaluating the technical, practical, and financial feasibility of proposed construction, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

Although under common ownership with Newbridge Construction Ltd., Strata operates independently, providing engineering assessments and advisory services to private developers, industrial enterprises, and public-sector clients.

### Project History (1996–2000)

- **1996 — Capital Industrial Site Assessment:** Preliminary engineering assessment of a proposed industrial development, including ground conditions, site layout, and infrastructure requirements.
- **1996–1997 — Capital Industrial Estate Expansion:** Site feasibility studies, preliminary engineering designs, and infrastructure planning in support of Newbridge's construction activities.
- **1997 — Piranburgh Warehouse Expansion Study:** Structural feasibility and construction cost assessments for the proposed expansion of an existing freight-storage facility.
- **1997–1998 — East Piranburgh Logistics Centre:** Preliminary site evaluation, engineering feasibility studies, and technical planning for a logistics development subsequently constructed by Newbridge.
- **1997–1998 — Advanced Powertrain Research Centre:** Structural and foundation assessments, utility-capacity reviews, and preliminary engineering recommendations supporting facility expansion undertaken by Newbridge.
- **1998–1999 — Industrial Facility Rehabilitation Assessments:** Evaluation of aging factory structures, foundations, and utility systems to determine feasible repair and modernization options.
- **1999 — Piranburgh Brownfield Redevelopment Study:** Assessment of a former industrial property, including ground conditions, environmental constraints, and potential redevelopment costs.
- **1999–2000 — Chuyoda Hospitality Group Second Resort Study:** Preliminary infrastructure and site-feasibility assessments contributing to Chuyoda's evaluation of a possible second resort property. No construction authorization or site selection is implied.
- **1999–2000 — Piranburgh Manufacturing Facility Modernization:** Structural assessments, preliminary engineering recommendations, and technical support for modernization works carried out by Newbridge.
- **2000 — Regional Freight Terminal Feasibility Study:** Preliminary assessment of a proposed freight terminal, covering site suitability, access infrastructure, drainage, and estimated construction requirements.
